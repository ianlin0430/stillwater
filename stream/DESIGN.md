# Design

## Physical scene
The user sits beside a softly lit Mac in the evening and watches a clear stream pool between other tasks; narrow dark moss-tinted controls frame warm natural artwork without dominating it.

## Visual system
The user selected **C — Soft Pixel** on September 22, 2026. This supersedes the original naturalistic painted direction. Use crisp pixel clusters, soft sage/jade water and warm sand, gently rounded cobalt creatures, restrained highlights and simplified shell detail. Avoid photorealistic speckles, coarse retro blocks, blur, giant eyes and human expressions. Character quality still depends on recognizable anatomy and coordinated movement.

Restrained UI around the pixel scene: ivory text, muted sage secondary labels, slate-green opaque control surfaces and a pale blue selection accent. No glass blur or permanent sidebar. Compact inspector appears only after selection. System-font text stays smooth and readable outside the pixel-rendered world.

## Scene
16:10 logical canvas, side-on water with left/right rock shelters and a central nursery. Native window starts at 1200×750. Render the world into a bounded 640×360 viewport with nearest-neighbor display, retaining 1280×720 simulation coordinates. Zoom 1–1.65. Red shrimp, silver threadfins and marbled hatchetfish (crayfish removed 2026-09-22). Articulated limbs and deforming fin/body meshes. Respect species silhouettes; no permanent mirrored sprite flips. Turns lead with the head and fold the body through a bend rather than squashing it like a card. Judge motion frame by frame at true pixel scale as well as at 1.65×.
