# Stillwater Stream

## Register
product

## Users
A personal Mac user observing an intimate aquatic world, often beside other work, who values detailed animals and a quiet computer above scale or simulation complexity.

## Product Purpose
One autonomous side-on stream pool with 14 initial individuals: shrimp and two fish species. Observation, modest zoom, and inspection only. The user selected soft pixel art (comparison C), replacing the earlier realistic painted direction. Quality means distinct characters with articulated movement that feels grounded and alive. Life continues through bounded offline progression without a background service.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, intimate, natural. A small window into living water.

## Anti-references
The preceding overhead wetland prototype with hundreds of tiny schematic animals. Dashboard sidebars, dense charts, care chores, cartoon faces, sliding flat cutouts, loud water effects, and uncapped rendering.

## Design Principles
1. Polish shrimp and fish movement before adding anything else.
2. Animal detail takes priority over environment spectacle.
3. Quiet sustained operation is a feature.
4. Observation never alters ecological outcomes.
5. Show a little; let the world carry the experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable system-font labels, keyboard focus, Tab selection, Escape dismissal, keyboard zoom, non-color status text, and a viewing light that does not change biology. Pause is observational and resumes without backlog.
