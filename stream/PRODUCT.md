# Stillwater Reef

## Register
product

## Users
A person who wants a quiet living aquarium beside their work, without care chores, accounts or a background service.

## Product purpose
Open a small 2D pixel reef and leave it to live. Twelve fish begin the world: six green chromis, two clownfish, two seahorses and two royal grammas. Fish grow, breed and quietly age; neglect never causes starvation or an empty aquarium. Habitat caps total eighteen.

Reef and Shipwreck garden share the same individuals, lineage, ecology and save. Fixed decoration slots provide physical obstacles, shelters and hitch contacts. Decoration changes do not alter food supply or population caps. The anemone and hitch plant can change style but cannot be removed.

## Personality
Quiet, intimate, gently alive. Animal silhouettes and readable movement carry the experience. No soundtrack, management dashboards, care alarms or cartoon expressions.

## Principles
1. Observation requires no care. Feeding is optional and capped.
2. Preserve each fish's identity through scene changes and save/load.
3. Make movement natural and easy to watch: shoaling, nestling, upright drifting and cave retreat.
4. Render crisply at integer pixel scales; respect foreground, background and hidden operation.
5. Keep controls small and out of the water. No fish inspector.
6. Protect existing saves. The new reef uses its own v3 world; older worlds remain untouched.

## Interaction and accessibility
Scene and decoration selectors, optional food, glass tapping, pointer curiosity, pause, viewing light, integer zoom and fullscreen. Use readable system text, keyboard focus, explicit labels and visible focus states. Viewing light changes presentation only. Pause resumes without an accumulated backlog.
